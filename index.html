WHAT IS THEKUA?

Thekua is a beloved traditional Indian sweet snack, especially famous in Bihar and nearby regions. Made with wheat flour, jaggery, ghee, and coconut, it is known for its rich taste and crunchy texture.

Prepared during festivals and family occasions, Thekua carries the warmth of homemade tradition and authentic flavors passed through generations.
